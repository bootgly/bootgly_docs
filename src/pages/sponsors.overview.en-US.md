## Why Sponsor Bootgly

Bootgly is a native, zero-dependency PHP framework — one async core for the Web and the Console — released under the MIT license and maintained by Rodrigo Vieira and contributors.

Sponsoring pays for the time that goes into the framework, its benchmarks and this documentation, and it puts your brand in front of the developers who read them.

## Sponsor Tiers

Your logo shows under the table of contents of every documentation page: in the right column on desktop, in the contents drawer on tablets and in the contents dialog on phones. The panel on this page shows the placement live.

| Tier | Placement | Logo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Platinum** | A wide logo at the top of the panel, one per row | 3:1 — send 600×200 |
| **Gold** | A square logo, two per row | 1:1 — send 300×300 |

Every logo links to your site. Send an SVG, or a transparent PNG with some padding inside the image; a separate version for the dark theme is welcome.

## Advertising

The ad card above the content of every documentation page — an image, a title, a short text and a link — is also available. Each page always shows the same creative, and different pages show different creatives. The card at the top of this page shows the placement live.

- **Image**: 4:3 — send 256×192.
- **Title**: one line.
- **Text**: up to about 100 characters.

## Become a Sponsor

- **Platinum, Gold and ads** — write to [public@bootgly.com](mailto:public@bootgly.com) with your company, the placement you want and the period. We reply with availability and terms.
- **Individual support** — [sponsor Bootgly on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/bootgly/).

## What We Accept

Sponsors and ads relevant to developers: hosting, tools, services, courses, books, events and jobs. We decline gambling, adult content, crypto schemes, malware and anything misleading, and we may decline any placement that does not fit the project.

## Our Commitments

- Sponsor logos and ads are labelled as such, and their links carry `rel="sponsored"`.
- No third-party scripts, cookies or tracking: the docs serve your image and your link, nothing else.
- Sponsorship never changes what the documentation says.
